### [1] AICTE Approval Process Handbook (APH 2024–2027)
- **Author / Organization:** All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi.
- **Title:** *Approval Process Handbook (APH 2024–2027): Norms and Procedures for Technical Institutions.*
- **Statutory Basis:** Chapter I & II (Grant of Approval) and Appendix 8 (Norms for Security Deposit / Corpus Fund).
- **URL:** [https://www.aicte-india.org/sites/default/files/APH%202024-27.pdf](https://www.aicte-india.org/sites/default/files/APH%202024-27.pdf)
- **Access Date:** 30 October 2026
- **Status:** **VERIFIED (Primary Regulatory Source)**
- **Key Takeaways:**
  1. Mandates that any Trust/Society/Section 8 Company establishing a new technical institution or expanding programs must submit a statutory **Security Deposit in the form of a Fixed Deposit Receipt (FDR)** pledged jointly with the **Regional Officer, AICTE**.
  2. Statutory lock-in tenure is typically fixed for **8 to 10 years (120 months)** as a performance guarantee for educational continuity.
  3. The deposit is held as collateral against non-compliance, unauthorized closure, or student/faculty fee defaults. Accrued interest is earmarked for Council faculty/student development funds.
  4. Release of the deposit upon completion of tenure requires: permanent campus operation, clearance of all student grievances, absence of pending legal or vigilance disputes, and approval from the **Competent Authority**.

### [5] Reserve Bank of India (RBI) IFSC Directory & Routing Architecture
- **Author / Organization:** Reserve Bank of India (RBI) / National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI).
- **Title:** *Indian Financial System Code (IFSC) Structure and Master Branch Directory.*
- **Statutory Basis:** RBI National Electronic Funds Transfer (NEFT) & RTGS System Regulations.
- **URL:** [https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/IFSCMICRDetails.aspx](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/IFSCMICRDetails.aspx)
- **Access Date:** 30 October 2026
- **Status:** **VERIFIED (Primary Financial Technical Standard)**
- **Key Takeaways:**
  1. The IFSC code is an 11-character alphanumeric string: characters 1–4 denote the bank code (`SBIN`, `PUNB`, `BARB`, `CNRB`, `UBIN`, `HDFC`, `ICIC`, `UTIB`), the 5th character is always `0` (reserved), and characters 6–11 denote the branch.
  2. Establishes a deterministic relationship between stated bank name and routing code: an instrument citing "State Bank of India" with an `HDFC` prefix represents a deterministic regulatory routing contradiction.

### [10] IETF RFC 6962 / RFC 9162: Certificate Transparency & Append-Only Logs
- **Author / Organization:** Internet Engineering Task Force (IETF) / B. Laurie, A. Langley, E. Kasper.
- **Title:** *RFC 6962: Certificate Transparency (Cryptographic Append-Only Merkle Tree Auditing).*
- **Statutory Basis:** IETF Standards Track RFC 6962 / RFC 9162.
- **URL:** [https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc6962](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc6962)
- **Access Date:** 30 October 2026
- **Status:** **VERIFIED (Primary Cryptographic Audit Standard)**
- **Key Takeaways:**
  1. Formulates the mathematical foundation of tamper-evident, append-only logs where each record cryptographically commits to the entire history of previous blocks via SHA-256 chaining.
  2. Demonstrates that any retrospective mutation, row deletion, or insertion breaks the chain sequence, providing cryptographic proof of tampering.
  3. Delivers immutable regulatory auditability without the latency, high carbon footprint, gas fees, or smart contract complexity of distributed public blockchains.

### [13] Intelligent Document Processing (IDP) & Financial OCR Standards
- **Author / Organization:** Intelligent Document Processing Standards & Vision Research (IEEE / arXiv).
- **Title:** *Field-Level vs. Character-Level Accuracy and Confidence Thresholding in Administrative Financial Document OCR.*
- **Statutory Basis:** Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition / IDP Frameworks.
- **URL:** [https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.01848](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.01848)
- **Access Date:** 30 October 2026
- **Status:** **VERIFIED (Primary Technical Vision Standard)**
- **Key Takeaways:**
  1. Highlights the "confident and wrong" paradox: OCR vision models frequently assign $>90\%$ raw character confidence to degraded document scans while misreading critical digits (e.g., misreading ₹35,00,000 as ₹3,50,000).
  2. Best practice mandates a **two-tier architecture**: (a) visual bounding box mapping with per-field confidence meters for human-in-the-loop inspection, paired with (b) **deterministic post-OCR business rule validation** (date chronological validation, IFSC prefix verification, amount norm matching).

## 3. Related Work & Government Platform Gap Analysis

| **GeM (Government e-Marketplace)** | Public procurement of goods and services | Integrates e-PBGs via bank SFMS messages; automated expiry tracking for contract durations. | Tailored to contractor purchase orders (1–3 years); lacks multi-tier educational regulatory lock-ins (10 years) and dual-source provenance matching. |
| **PFMS (Public Financial Management System)** | Central expenditure tracking and fund disbursements | Direct benefit transfers (DBT) and treasury ledgering; Maker-Checker-Approver workflows. | Focuses on outbound expenditure and grants; lacks specialized regulatory document OCR intake, lien validation, and cross-institutional duplicate FDR detection. |
